ℹ️ How it works

  • The rules are checked before Lumo thinks about a reply, so a channel it stays quiet in costs nothing.
  • It still answers when you @mention it or reply to it, wherever the rules allow. It also answers to its name in a message, except in channels set to “only when mentioned”.
  • Direct messages and the daily and monthly limits are not affected.
  • Music, games, giveaways and polls work in every channel as before. This only controls AI chat.

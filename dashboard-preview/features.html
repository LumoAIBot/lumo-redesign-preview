Fun & Games

  • 🌌On

    Starkeepers

    Lumo's night-sky game (beta, actively updated): members catch stars each night, grow their own sky and light constellations together.

    42 stargazers so far

    Free, no AI. Stardust comes from chatting and games, never from coins.

    Open the server sky
    /sky watch
  • 🎲On

    Games

    Blackjack, slots, poker, Connect Four and more, for play-money coins.

    /game
  • 🪙On

    Lumo Coins

    One play-money wallet, a daily claim and your server's top 10.

    Open Economy
    /coins
  • 🎵On

    Music

    Play SoundCloud songs in your voice channel.

    /play lofi beats

Community

  • 👋On

    Welcome

    Greets new members with a welcome card and your own words.

    #welcome

    Customize
  • 🎭On

    Join role

    Gives every new member a role automatically.

    @Member

    Turning it off clears the saved role.

    Change role
  • 🏷️Set up

    Reaction roles

    Members pick their roles by tapping an emoji.

    Create a panel
  • 🌈Set up

    Color roles

    A ready-made set of name colors members can pick.

    Add color roles
  • 🎉On

    Giveaways

    Run a giveaway: members press a button to enter and Lumo picks fair winners.

    None running right now

    Free, no AI. Up to 10 at a time.

    Manage giveaways
    /giveaway start
  • 📊On

    Polls

    Ask a question with up to 10 options; members vote with buttons and watch the counts live.

    Free, no AI. Votes are never shown by name.

    Manage polls
    /poll create
  • 🌟Off

    Level-up messages & roles

    Everyone already earns XP and levels here (/rank, /top). Turn this on to post level-ups and give level roles.

    Off by default. XP, levels and /rank work either way.

    Open Levels
    /rank
  • 📅Set up

    Date channel

    A voice channel that shows today's date, renamed at midnight in your server's time zone (set on the Settings page).

    Time zone
    /setup datechannel

Chat & AI

  • 💬On

    Chat with Lumo

    Mention it in any channel and ask anything. It reads pictures and searches the web.

  • 🧭Off

    AI channels

    Choose which channels Lumo answers in, and give a channel its own tone.

    Off means the default: it answers everywhere it can see.

    Choose channels
  • 🖼️On

    Images

    Ask for a picture, or have it edit one you share.

  • 🎬On

    Video

    3-second AI video clips, made from a prompt.

  • 🎙️On

    Live voice

    Talk with Lumo in a voice channel and hear it answer.

    /voice
  • 🎨On

    Customize

    Give Lumo its own name, personality, avatar and banner in this server.

    Open Customize

Admin

  • 📜Needs attention

    Server logs

    A private record of joins, edits and deletes for your moderators.

    Lumo can’t post in #mod-log.

    Turning it off switches all 25 events off. The channel stays saved.

    Fix it
  • 🛡️On

    Permissions

    Checks that Lumo is allowed to do its job here.

  • ✦On

    Premium

    Unlocks the AI for this server ($4.99/month, one plan per server).

    Active until Nov 24 (sample)

    /premium status
  • 🕳️Set up

    Void

    A bait channel. A post there is deleted and logged. Admins are left alone.

    Free. Starts as log only. Timeout, kick, or softban are optional.

    /setup void
  • 🚨On

    AutoMod

    Removes invite links, scam links, mass mentions and spam bursts. Each rule has its own switch.

    Open AutoMod
  • 🧹On

    Clean up messages

    Delete up to 100 recent messages in a channel.

    Administrators only. Lumo asks you to confirm first.

    /purge

Everything here also works with slash commands in Discord. Most switches only turn things off; to turn those on you choose a channel or role in Settings. Level-up messages & roles and Starkeepers turn on and off right here.

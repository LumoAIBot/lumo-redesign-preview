💡 How coins work

  • New players start with ✦ 5,000.
  • Claim your daily with /coins daily: ✦ 2,000 once per Eastern day (it resets at midnight ET).
  • Claim on consecutive days for a streak: from day 2 you get +100 extra per day, up to +500 (from day 6). Miss a day and it starts over at day 1.
  • Wallets are shared across servers: one wallet follows you everywhere. This board only lists members of this server.
  • The house pays the winners. Each server has its own house wallet, which starts at ✦ 100,000,000. Players are always paid in full, and the house bottoms out at 0 and refills from losses.
  • Lumo Coins are play money only: no cash-out, no transfers, no real-world value.

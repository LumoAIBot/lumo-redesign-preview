🏅 Level roles

Reach the level, get the role. Lumo’s own role must be above these roles in Server Settings → Roles, and it needs Manage Roles.

Level roles are only given while level-up messages & roles is on.

  • Level 5@Regular
  • Level 10@Night Owl
  • Level 20@Stargazer

Level 1 to 200. Saving a level again replaces its role. Lumo only adds roles; it never removes any.

🚫 No XP in these channels

For example a spam or bot-commands channel. Threads and forum posts follow their channel. This always applies, whether level-up messages are on or off.

Channels

ℹ️ How it works

  • 15–25 XP for a message, at most once a minute per member, and 10 XP for every minute in voice with someone else. Bots, webhooks and DMs never count.
  • Level 1 takes 100 XP; each next level takes a bit more (5 × level² + 50 × level + 100). Level 5 is about an hour of chatting, level 10 about four.
  • The same level shows on /rank, /top, the /profile activity card and the Level Up badge.
  • Only XP numbers are stored, never what anyone wrote. /forgetme erases this server’s XP.

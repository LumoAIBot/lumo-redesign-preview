AutoMod

Light, automatic clean-up. Every rule is off until you turn it on.

A caught message is deleted, the member gets a short notice that deletes itself, and an optional timeout. Never a kick or ban. Catches are recorded in your log channel.

Invite linksScam & phishing linksMass mentionsSpam burstsExempt

🔗 Invite links

On

Removes Discord invites to other servers. Invites to this server (and its vanity link) are fine.

🎣 Scam & phishing links

On

Removes links to known scam and phishing sites (a free public list, refreshed twice a day) and obvious fakes like discord-gift or steamcommunlty links.

📢 Mass mentions

On

Removes a message that pings more than this many members or roles. @everyone and @here count when the member isn’t allowed to use them.

💨 Spam bursts

Off

Removes messages when someone sends too many too fast, or the same message 3 times in 30 seconds.

🛡️ Exempt

  • Always exempt: Administrators, Manage Server, Manage Messages, bots and webhooks.
Exempt roles
Exempt channels

ℹ️ How it works

  • Lumo needs Manage Messages to delete, and Moderate Members for timeouts.
  • Message text is only read to check these rules. Nothing is stored.
  • A removed message earns no XP and never gets an AI reply.

A Discord bot · Starkeepers in beta

A night sky that lives in your Discord server.

Every night a new batch of stars turns up. Catch one, keep your watch streak going, and fill in constellations with the rest of your server. Every so often a wild star shows up, and you hunt it down as a team.

Free to add · Nothing to install

The moon phase changes which stars turn upEclipses bring rare ones
01

One star a night, if you show up.

Run /sky watch once a night. Do the three little nightly tasks, keep the streak alive, and check your collection with /sky view. Miss a night and the streak resets, so maybe set a reminder.

02

Sometimes a star fights back.

Bring your three best stars to a hunt. Specials charge on the third move, ultimates on the eighth. Win, and the wild star might decide to stay.
